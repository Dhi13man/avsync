# Code of Conduct

Audio Video Sync Lab should be useful to people who are trying to solve a
media-sync problem, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct, respectful, and specific.
- Assume reports are made in good faith unless there is clear evidence
  otherwise.
- Critique ideas, code, and behavior rather than people.
- Keep discussions scoped to the project and its users.
- Credit prior work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, slurs, or sustained personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention in project spaces.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, repeated derailment, or bad-faith demands for maintainer labor.
- Security disclosure pressure, public exploit drops, or attempts to bypass
  the reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or ban
accounts when behavior makes the project less safe or less productive.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainer privately through
the GitHub repository owner profile. Include links, screenshots, dates, and any
other context that helps establish what happened.

## Attribution

This policy is aligned with the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), while keeping
the text short enough for this project.
